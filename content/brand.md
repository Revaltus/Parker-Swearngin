# About Parker Swearngin LLC

## Identity

Parker Swearngin LLC (the firm's story also refers to it as Parker Swearngin, LLP) is a CPA-led accounting, tax, payroll, and advisory firm. Its main office is in Lee's Summit, Missouri, and it has a new office in Blue Springs. The firm describes itself as one of the leading firms in Lee's Summit and a staple of the area's business community. It is a member of the Lee's Summit Chamber of Commerce.

**Tagline:** "Stay on a healthy financial path with dedicated, strategic advisory services."

**Mission:** Help clients maintain financial viability in the present while taking a proactive approach to achieve future goals. This requires open communication to understand each client's needs through research and sound analysis. The firm is dedicated to high standards of excellence and professionalism.

**Values:** competent and timely advice, responsiveness, hard work, and esteem earned in the community.

**Who the firm serves:** Local individuals and businesses seeking personalized financial guidance, including tax, bookkeeping, payroll, and advisory support. The firm's expertise covers:
- tax returns for individuals, partnerships, trusts, and corporations
- business consulting and new company setup
- IRS examinations
- payroll and bookkeeping for large and small businesses

**Team:** The team includes CPAs and tax professionals, several with more than 20 years of experience. It works together to provide competent and timely advice.

**Geography:** The firm is local in scope. It serves Kansas City, MO (KC), the Kansas City metro area, Kansas, Missouri, and the surrounding area.

## Positioning & Differentiation

**Positioning statement:** A dedicated team that gives every client close analysis and personal attention, with bundled services at a fixed monthly fee so there are no billing surprises and tax compliance stays stress-free.

**What makes the firm distinctive:**
- It combines expertise, experience, and a team mentality so every client receives close analysis and attention.
- It hires seasoned tax professionals.
- Clients return year after year. The firm credits its high standards and work ethic.
- Services are bundled for a fixed monthly fee to avoid billing surprises.
- Clients get a responsive team with a dedicated point of contact who understands their industry.

**Core services:**
- **Accounting:** daily transaction classification, expense tracking, and reporting. It also covers account reconciliation (bank accounts, credit cards, and electronic transactions into QuickBooks) so clients have a clear picture of their financial position.
- **Payroll:** processing scheduled payrolls, employee setup, salary changes, payroll filings and tax compliance, W2 and 1099 processing, and time tracking.
- **Business Tax:** tax preparation, tax planning, electronic filing, and year-round tax advice, delivered through a digital filing process.
- **Personal Income Tax:** a modern, secure, personalized filing process with digital document upload, signature and filing, customized support, and year-round advice for individuals and families.
- **Business advisory and consulting:** the firm advises on entity type analysis, accounting system setup, retirement plan analysis, and credit card rewards.

**What clients need, in the firm's words:**
- clean, current books and clear visibility into cash position
- reliable payroll, 1099, and W2 processing
- proactive year-round tax planning and compliance
- guidance on entity structure, retirement plans, and growth decisions
- predictable pricing

Clients' common pain points are uneven cash flow, little time for finances, unexpected tax bills, messy books, not knowing true profitability, and accountants who are unresponsive or don't understand their industry.

**How clients find the firm:** Referrals and search, among other channels.

## Industries Served

### Law Firms
**Who:** Attorneys and law practices.

**Value proposition:** A CPA-led team that handles books, payroll, and tax for the practice. The firm integrates Clio with QuickBooks, so billing and trust activity flow into clean books. Attorneys can focus on clients and get clear partner-level financials.

**Pain points:**
- trust accounting (IOLTA) and client-fund compliance
- irregular cash flow from contingency and billing cycles
- partner compensation and entity structure
- time spent on admin instead of billable work

**Common triggers:** starting or splitting a firm, a trust accounting concern, partner changes, or a current accountant who doesn't understand legal practice.

### Service-based Businesses
**Who:** Subcontractors, including HVAC, drywall, concrete, and asphalt businesses.

**Value proposition:** Clean books, payroll, and year-round tax planning from one team, so owners see their cash position clearly and stay compliant.

**Pain points:**
- uneven cash flow from slow-paying general contractors and progress billing
- making outgoing payments on time
- payroll and 1099 subcontractor tracking
- messy books that make tax time stressful

**Common triggers:** a cash crunch, a missed payment or payroll, a surprise tax bill, or growth that outpaces the current bookkeeper.

### Professional Services
**Who:** Engineers and architects.

**Value proposition:** Accounting, payroll, and proactive tax planning from one team, so principals see project profitability and keep more of what they earn.

**Pain points:**
- project-based, uneven revenue
- tracking work in progress (WIP) and project profitability
- owner compensation and entity structure
- payroll and R&D or other tax credit opportunities

**Common triggers:** firm growth, new partners or principals, a large project that strains cash flow, or a tax bill larger than expected.

### Real Estate Brokers
**Who:** Real estate brokers.

**Value proposition:** Cost segregation studies, along with accounting services tailored to real estate brokers.

**Pain points:**
- irregular, commission-based income
- estimated tax payments
- tracking deductible expenses
- missing tax-saving opportunities such as cost segregation studies

**Common triggers:** a big commission year with a large tax bill, brokerage or property growth, or an investment property purchase where cost segregation could help.

### Medical Professionals
**Who:** Medical professionals and practice owners.

**Value proposition:** Accounting, payroll, and tax handled by a responsive CPA-led team, so providers can focus on patients and get proactive planning and advice.

**Pain points:**
- little time for finances because patients come first
- complex tax situations (practice income, retirement plans, entity structure)
- payroll and compliance for clinical staff
- not knowing what the practice really earns

**Common triggers:** opening or buying a practice, a jump in income and tax liability, an unresponsive current accountant, or a need for entity or retirement planning.

### Veterinarians
**Who:** Veterinarians and veterinary practice owners.

**Value proposition:** Responsive, CPA-led accounting, payroll, and tax support, so practice owners can focus on animals and plan for growth.

**Pain points:**
- heavy payroll and staff costs
- equipment and facility purchases (depreciation, cost segregation)
- practice acquisition and valuation
- little time for finances beyond patient care

**Common triggers:** buying or opening a practice, major equipment or building purchases, partner buy-ins, or an accountant who doesn't know the veterinary industry.
