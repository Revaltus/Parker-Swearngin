# About Parker Swearngin LLC

## Identity

Parker Swearngin LLC is a CPA firm in Lee's Summit, Missouri. Its firm story refers to it as Parker Swearngin, LLP. The website is parkerswearngin.com, and its email domain is psllp.biz. The firm describes itself as one of the leading firms in Lee's Summit and a staple of the area's business community. It has also opened a new office in Blue Springs.

**Tagline:** "Stay on a healthy financial path with dedicated, strategic advisory services."

**What the firm does:** For years it has provided personalized financial guidance to local individuals and businesses. Its work includes:
- Tax returns for individuals, partnerships, trusts, and corporations
- Business consulting and new company setup
- IRS examinations
- Payroll and bookkeeping for large and small businesses

**Core services**
- **Accounting:** daily transaction classification, expense tracking, reporting, and account reconciliation (bank accounts, credit card accounts, and electronic transactions into QuickBooks).
- **Payroll:** scheduled payroll processing, employee setup, salary changes, payroll filings and tax compliance, W-2 and 1099 processing, and time tracking.
- **Business Tax:** tax preparation, electronic filing, tax planning, and year-round tax advice for businesses.
- **Personal Income Tax:** a secure digital process (document upload, signature, and filing) with customized support and year-round advice for individuals and families.
- **Business advisory and consulting:** the firm advises on entity type analysis, accounting system setup, retirement plan analysis, and credit card rewards.

**Team:** The team is CPA-led and works together to provide competent and timely advice. It includes CPAs, and one partner is also an attorney (JD, LL.M) certified for Federal Tax Court. Team members cite 21 to 25 years or more of experience in public accounting and the business.

**Mission:** Help clients maintain financial viability in the present while taking a proactive approach to achieving future goals. This relies on open communication to understand client needs, backed by research and sound analysis, and a commitment to high standards of excellence and professionalism.

**Values:** competent and timely advice, responsiveness, hard work, and esteem earned in the community.

**Tone of voice:** warm, approachable, and client-focused. It is professional but conversational, and it emphasizes trust, personal attention, and reduced stress. Descriptors are approachable, personalized, professional, reassuring, and client-focused. The aspirational tone is friendly and professional. **Differentiators**
- Expertise, experience, and a team mentality combine so every client receives close analysis and attention.

- Bundled services for a fixed monthly fee, which avoids billing surprises.
- Year-round tax planning and support, not just seasonal filing.
- Industry-aware service for specific professions, aimed at clients frustrated by accountants who are unresponsive or don't understand their industry.

**Common client needs**
- Clean, current books and clear visibility into cash position

- Proactive year-round tax planning and compliance

## Industries Served

### Law Firms
**Ideal clients:** Attorneys and law practices.

**Value proposition:** A CPA-led team that handles books, payroll, and tax for the practice. The firm integrates Clio with QuickBooks, so billing and trust activity flow into clean books. Attorneys can focus on clients and get clear partner-level financials.

**Pain points:**
- Trust accounting (IOLTA) and client-fund compliance
- Irregular cash flow from contingency and billing cycles
- Partner compensation and entity structure
- Time spent on admin instead of billable work

**Typical triggers:** starting or splitting a firm, a trust accounting concern, partner changes, or a current accountant who doesn't understand legal practice.

### Service-based Businesses
**Ideal clients:** Subcontractors, including HVAC, drywall, concrete, and asphalt.

**Value proposition:** Clean books, payroll, and year-round tax planning from one team, so owners see their cash position clearly and stay compliant.

**Pain points:**
- Uneven cash flow from slow-paying general contractors and progress billing
- Making outgoing payments on time
- Payroll and 1099 subcontractor tracking
- Messy books that make tax time stressful

**Typical triggers:** a cash crunch, a missed payment or payroll, a surprise tax bill, or growth that outpaces the current bookkeeper.

### Professional Services
**Ideal clients:** Engineers and architects.

**Value proposition:** Accounting, payroll, and proactive tax planning from one team, so principals see project profitability and keep more of what they earn.

**Pain points:**
- Project-based, uneven revenue
- Tracking work in progress (WIP) and project profitability
- Owner compensation and entity structure
- Payroll and R&D or other tax credit opportunities

**Typical triggers:** firm growth, new partners or principals, a large project that strains cash flow, or a tax bill larger than expected.

### Real Estate Brokers
**Ideal clients:** Real estate brokers.

**Value proposition:** Cost segregation studies, along with accounting and tax services tailored to brokers.

**Pain points:**
- Irregular, commission-based income
- Estimated tax payments
- Tracking deductible expenses
- Missing tax-saving opportunities such as cost segregation studies

**Typical triggers:** a big commission year with a large tax bill, brokerage or property growth, or an investment property purchase where cost segregation could help.

### Medical Professionals
**Ideal clients:** Medical professionals and practice owners.

**Value proposition:** Accounting, payroll, and tax handled by a responsive CPA-led team, so providers can focus on patients and get proactive planning and advice.

**Pain points:**
- Little time for finances because patients come first
- Complex tax situations, including practice income, retirement plans, and entity structure
- Payroll and compliance for clinical staff
- Not knowing what the practice really earns

**Typical triggers:** opening or buying a practice, a jump in income and tax liability, an unresponsive current accountant, or a need for entity or retirement planning.

### Veterinarians
**Ideal clients:** Veterinarians and veterinary practice owners.

**Value proposition:** Responsive, CPA-led accounting, payroll, and tax support, so practice owners can focus on animals and plan for growth.

**Pain points:**
- Heavy payroll and staff costs
- Equipment and facility purchases (depreciation, cost segregation)
- Practice acquisition and valuation
- Little time for finances beyond patient care

**Typical triggers:** buying or opening a practice, major equipment or building purchases, partner buy-ins, or an accountant who doesn't know the veterinary industry.
