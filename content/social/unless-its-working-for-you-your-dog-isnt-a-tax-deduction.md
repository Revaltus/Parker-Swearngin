## LinkedIn

A $2,550 guard dog bill can be a legitimate deduction. A $2,550 family dog bill never is.

Here is the difference. A concrete subcontractor south of Lee's Summit keeps about $250,000 of equipment in a fenced yard, and thieves have hit the neighboring yard twice. The owner buys a trained guard dog that lives and works at the yard. Food, vet care, training, and kennel supplies total $2,550 a year, worth roughly $765 in tax savings at a 30 percent combined rate.

The test is whether the dog is doing a job. A pet in your home office, a kennel bill during a business trip, or a dog walker is personal. A dog that guards the yard but also sleeps on your couch needs an honest allocation.

Service animals fall under medical expenses, but only if you itemize and clear the 7.5 percent of AGI floor.
