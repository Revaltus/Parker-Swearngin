## LinkedIn

A payroll deposit pushed back a few days draws an IRS failure-to-deposit penalty that starts at 2 percent and can climb to 10 percent or more.

Those slips rarely come from not knowing better. They come from an owner running on no sleep and no margin: a skipped third-quarter estimate, progress billing sent two weeks late, books three months behind, bids priced on gut feel.

Our latest post treats self-care for business owners as a financial safeguard, because your judgment is the one asset that never shows up on the balance sheet. The fix is structure. If fixed costs run $40,000 a month, a 60 to 90 day reserve is $80,000 to $120,000 of breathing room. That is what lets an HVAC or drywall owner in Lee's Summit turn down a bad job or wait out a general contractor who pays on day 75.

The post ends with a 30-day plan for KC service businesses, starting with one money hour a week.
