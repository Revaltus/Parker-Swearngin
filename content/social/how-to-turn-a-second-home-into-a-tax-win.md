## LinkedIn

A drywall subcontractor buys a $450,000 lake condo. The family stays 12 days, renters stay 120 and pay $24,000. On paper, that is a $6,100 rental loss.

Here is the catch. Rental losses are passive, and the $25,000 allowance starts shrinking at $100,000 of modified adjusted gross income and is gone at $150,000. For a subcontractor, engineer, or practice owner coming off a big year, that loss is likely suspended and carried forward instead of reducing business income.

The day count drives all of it. Rent 14 days or fewer and the income is tax-free. Rent 15 or more with personal use over the greater of 14 days or 10% of rental days, and deductions are capped at rental income.
