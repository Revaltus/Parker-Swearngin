## LinkedIn

A $10,000 balance carried for one month at 24% APR costs about $200 in interest. Earning 2% rewards on that same $10,000 of spending also yields $200. Net result: nothing earned and a new debt.

That is one of the checks in our new post for KC service businesses. Another one surprised us: for a hypothetical HVAC sub spending $22,700 a month, a flat 2% card earns about $5,448 a year, while a 5% fuel / 1% other card earns about $4,404. The flashy category rate left roughly $1,044 on the table.

For HVAC, drywall, concrete, and asphalt subs in Lee's Summit, the bigger win is often timing. A statement cycle can give you about 48 days before cash leaves your account, which lines up with a GC paying on net-45. We also cover why card payments to other subs go on a 1099-K instead of a 1099-NEC, and how rewards are generally treated for tax purposes.
