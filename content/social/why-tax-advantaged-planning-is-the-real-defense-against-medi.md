## LinkedIn

A Lee's Summit HVAC owner has two GCs 60 days behind on payment when a spouse's emergency surgery lands a $14,000 bill. The business account is tight. The personal savings account is thinner than it should be.

Most emergency fund advice skips this: a cash cushion is built with after-tax dollars, and in a service business it quietly turns into working capital the moment a slow-paying GC stretches your cash.

The stronger move is to find your health plan's out-of-pocket maximum (up to $18,400 for family coverage in 2025), then fund an HSA first and a separate cash reserve second. A family HSA contribution of $8,550 is worth roughly $2,400 in federal and Missouri income tax, and it works whether or not you itemize. The itemized medical deduction only helps once expenses pass 7.5 percent of AGI and your total itemized deductions beat the $31,500 standard deduction.
