## LinkedIn

Most owners can name every policy they carry. Few can say what a bad year would cost them.

Our latest post walks through an illustrative HVAC subcontractor in Lee's Summit: $2.4 million in revenue, 14 employees, six trucks, about $140,000 in fixed costs a month, and roughly $90,000 in cash. Moving the truck deductible from $1,000 to $5,000 trims the premium by about $1,800 a year, and one claim would not threaten payroll. But the general liability limit only matches the GC contract minimum. One serious injury claim could put equipment and home equity on the table.

The fix is to carry small, predictable risks yourself and redirect the savings to higher liability limits or an umbrella policy. Total spend stays about the same, and protection lands where the damage would be.

For KC service business owners, sizing coverage is a CPA question as much as an agent question, because the books, the tax return, and the cash position already hold the answer.
