## LinkedIn

A $58,000 heavy-duty pickup can produce about $15,660 in first-year tax savings. Leased, the same truck saves about $2,754. So buying wins, right?

Not automatically. In our side-by-side for an HVAC owner (90% business use, a 30% combined federal and Missouri rate, Section 179 up to $52,200), buying did give the bigger first-year break. But saving $15,660 still means spending $52,200. The deduction only helps if the business has taxable income to absorb it and you would buy the truck anyway.

Mileage often settles the question sooner. A tech running calls from Lee's Summit to Raymore, Blue Springs, and downtown KC can log 28,000 miles a year. On a 36-month lease with a 12,000-mile allowance, that is roughly $12,000 in overage at 25 cents a mile.
