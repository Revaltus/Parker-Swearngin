## LinkedIn

Three years of nursing home care at $9,500 a month comes to $342,000. Medicare covers short skilled recovery stays, not months of custodial help.

For an HVAC, drywall, concrete, or asphalt owner, that bill is harder to plan for. Progress billing and slow-paying customers make income lumpy, and the company may not sell in the same year a health event hits.

Our post compares traditional and hybrid long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance, and dedicated savings. It also explains how premiums are reported differently for a sole proprietor, an LLC partner, and an S-corp owner holding more than 2 percent of the shares.

A quick test: write down where each dollar of a $350,000 care bill would come from if it arrived next year. If the answer is "the business sale," there is work to do.
