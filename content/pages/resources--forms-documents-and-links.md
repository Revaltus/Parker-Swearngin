## New business and accounting setup forms

Starting a company, or restructuring one, raises questions that need answers before anything is filed. For new company setup and entity formation documents in Missouri, our team asks for a short list: the proposed business name, each owner and ownership percentage, the business address, a registered agent, and whether you already have an EIN. Kansas setups need much the same information.

Order matters here. Entity Type Analysis comes first, because choosing between an LLC, an S-corp, or another structure shapes which formation documents you file and how you pay yourself. It is one of the business advisory and consulting services our CPA team provides.

Then comes Accounting System Setup: a chart of accounts built around your business, bank and credit card connections, and expense tracking that starts with your first transaction. If growth has outpaced your current bookkeeper, this is also a clean reset. Questions about any of it? [Send us a note](/contact), or read about our [accounting services](/services/accounting).

## Payroll forms for new hires, raises and contractors

Payroll is the task owners most want off their plate, and the paperwork behind it is where mistakes start. Our payroll service covers employee setup, scheduled payroll processing, salary changes, time tracking, payroll filings, and W2 and 1099 processing. Here is when each form shows up:

- **W-4:** Every new employee completes one before the first payroll. It tells us how much federal tax to withhold, and a fresh one is needed if their situation changes.
- **W-9:** Every independent contractor completes one before you pay them. It supplies the name and taxpayer ID needed for their 1099.
- **Raises and salary changes:** Send the new rate and effective date, and it is updated before the next payroll runs.
- **Time tracking:** Hours flow into payroll so hourly pay matches the work actually done.

Classification matters. Calling someone a 1099 contractor when they should be a W-2 employee can lead to back taxes and penalties. HVAC, drywall, concrete, and asphalt subcontractors who pay 1099 subs feel this most, since every sub needs a W-9 on file before the first check goes out. You can find the IRS versions of these forms in the links below, and our team will tell you which one applies. See our [payroll services](/services/payroll) for the full picture.

## Tax organizers and checklists for year-round planning

Paperwork gathered in March is a scramble. Paperwork gathered as you go is a plan. These checklists cover personal and business returns together, and they feed the year-round tax advice that comes with our tax services.

- Personal filers: W-2s, 1099s, brokerage and retirement account statements, mortgage interest, and property tax records.
- Self-employed filers: income records from every client or platform, mileage logs, home office details, and health insurance premiums.
- Quarterly estimated tax: last year's return, year-to-date profit, and confirmations of payments already made.
- Deduction documentation: dated receipts or card statements with a note on business purpose, plus invoices for equipment purchases.
- Retirement Plan Analysis: current plan statements, owner and employee compensation, and headcount.
- Credit Card Rewards: business card statements, so rewards and expenses are tracked correctly.

Retirement Plan Analysis and Credit Card Rewards are two advisory topics where the right documents change how much you save. Learn more about our [business tax](/services/tax) and [personal income tax](/services/personal-tax-prep-planning) services.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Forms documents and links

**Q: How do I send my tax documents to a CPA in Lee's Summit securely?**
A: Skip email attachments and use our secure digital document upload. Our team reviews what you send and tells you if anything is missing. Then you sign electronically and we file electronically. If an upload fails, contact the team through the contact page and someone will help you directly.

**Q: When does a business need a W-4 versus a W-9?**
A: Collect a W-4 from every new employee before their first payroll, because it sets federal withholding. Collect a W-9 from every independent contractor before you pay them, because it supplies the taxpayer ID needed for their 1099. Getting the 1099 versus W-2 call wrong can lead to back taxes and penalties, so ask before you hire.

**Q: What information do I need to start a business in Missouri?**
A: Expect to share the proposed business name, each owner and ownership percentage, the business address, and how owners will be paid. Before anything is filed, our Entity Type Analysis compares structures such as an LLC or S-corp. We also set up your accounting system so the books are clean from day one.

**Q: What documents do self-employed filers need for quarterly estimated taxes?**
A: Gather last year's return, year-to-date income records, and a running list of deductible expenses such as mileage, home office, and equipment. Estimated payments are due quarterly, so we use those records to project what you owe, then plan the right payment before each deadline instead of facing one large bill in April.

**Q: Do you work with clients in Kansas as well as Missouri?**
A: Yes. Parker Swearngin serves Kansas City, MO, the KC metro, and clients across Kansas and Missouri. That is why this page links to both the Missouri and Kansas Departments of Revenue, along with the IRS. Most paperwork is handled through our digital upload, so location rarely slows anything down.
