## Frequently Asked Questions About Personal income tax

**Q: How much does personal income tax preparation cost in Lee's Summit?**
A: Pricing depends on the complexity of your return: W2 income alone is simpler than a return with 1099 income, rental property, or a business. For clients who also use us for business tax, accounting, or payroll, we can bundle everything into one fixed monthly fee, so there are no billing surprises.

**Q: What documents do I need to bring for my tax return?**
A: Bring your prior-year return and every income document you received: W2s, 1099s, and brokerage or retirement account statements. Add records of deductions, such as mortgage interest, charitable gifts, and childcare costs. You upload them through our digital process, and the team tells you if anything is missing before the return is prepared.

**Q: Do I need to make estimated quarterly tax payments?**
A: Likely yes if you're self-employed, earn commissions, or have income without withholding, such as 1099 work or investment gains. Federal estimates are generally due four times a year. We calculate them from your actual year-to-date numbers, so you set aside the right amount instead of guessing.

**Q: Can you prepare both Missouri and Kansas returns?**
A: Yes. We serve individuals and owners across Kansas City, the KC Metro, Missouri, and Kansas, so a return that touches both states is familiar territory. We prepare federal and state returns together and handle electronic filing. Tell us where you live and where you earn, and the team sorts out the credits and filing requirements for each state.

**Q: What should I do if I receive an IRS notice?**
A: Don't ignore it, and don't respond before you understand it. Send us the notice and the team reviews what the IRS is asking, gathers supporting records, and prepares a response before the deadline printed on the letter. Our history includes IRS examination support, and the team includes a tax attorney for disputes.
