## Check your federal and Missouri refund status in minutes

You filed your return, and now you want to know where the money is. Think of this page as your tax refund tracker for Lee's Summit MO and the wider KC Metro. Four steps, no hold music, and a personal income tax refund tracker is only a few clicks away.

### Gather your return
Pull up the copy of the return you filed. You will need the Social Security number or ITIN, your filing status, and the exact refund amount in whole dollars.

### Check the IRS Where's My Refund tool
Visit the [IRS refund page](https://www.irs.gov/refunds) and choose Where's My Refund. Enter those three details to see whether your return was received, approved, or sent.

### Check the Missouri Return Tracker
Open the [Missouri Return Tracker](https://mytax.mo.gov/rptp/portal/home/return-tracker) from the Missouri Department of Revenue and enter the information it asks for. Federal and state refunds move separately, so check both.

### Kansas filers across the KC Metro
Live or work on the Kansas side of State Line? Your Kansas refund status comes from the [Kansas Department of Revenue](https://www.ksrevenue.gov), not from Missouri.

## What to have in hand before you look up your refund

A two-minute search turns into a twenty-minute hunt if the details are missing. Gather these first.

**For individual returns**

- Social Security number or ITIN
- Filing status (single, married filing jointly, head of household)
- The exact refund amount shown on your return

**For business returns**

- Employer Identification Number (EIN)
- Entity type, such as LLC, S-corp, partnership, or C-corp
- Return type and the date it was filed

The IRS individual tool is not built for business refunds, so a business tax refund status check usually takes a different route. If you are a client and do not have these details, ask the team. We can pull them from your e-filed return and help you follow up with the IRS or Missouri.

## Why a refund can take longer than expected

A late refund is rarely a sign that something is wrong. Most delays come from a short list of causes, and nearly all of them can be fixed.

- **E-file errors.** A mistyped Social Security number or a name that does not match IRS records can stall a return.
- **Identity verification.** The IRS or Missouri may ask you to confirm who you are before releasing money.
- **Amended returns.** A corrected return is reviewed separately and follows its own track.
- **Math or credit mismatches.** If a credit or a figure does not line up with what the agency has on file, the return gets a second look.
- **Notices waiting on you.** A letter sitting unopened is the most avoidable delay of all.

**Q: How do I check my tax refund status in Lee's Summit, MO?**
A: Use the IRS Where's My Refund tool for your federal refund and the Missouri Return Tracker for your state refund. Both ask for your Social Security number or ITIN, filing status, and the exact refund amount. Kansas filers use the Kansas Department of Revenue. If something looks off, our Lee's Summit team can review your return with you.

**Q: Can I check a business tax refund status online?**
A: The IRS Where's My Refund tool is built for individual returns, so business refunds usually need a different route. Have your EIN, entity type, return type, and filing date ready. Clients can ask our team to pull those details from the e-filed return and help follow up with the IRS or Missouri.

**Q: Why is my refund taking longer than expected?**
A: Common causes include e-file errors, identity verification, an amended return, a math or credit mismatch, or a notice waiting on your response. None of these automatically signal a serious problem. If you received a letter from the IRS or Missouri, send it to our team before replying, since our IRS Examinations experience helps us read it correctly.

**Q: Do payroll tax overpayments get refunded?**
A: Sometimes. An overpayment of payroll taxes may be refunded or applied as a credit to a future period, depending on how it happened and what is filed to correct it. Our payroll team handles processing and filings, so we can review your records and recommend which route fits.
