## Your return, delivered safely instead of through an email attachment

A tax return holds everything a stranger would want: Social Security numbers, EINs, bank account details, payroll figures. Sending it as an email attachment is the weakest way to move it. Inboxes get forwarded, synced to old phones, and sometimes broken into.

SafeSend Returns is the secure delivery step the team uses instead. You get a notice, sign in through a secure login, review your return, and sign it right there. There are no attachments to hunt for and nothing new to learn.

If you run a busy practice or business in Lee's Summit or anywhere in the Kansas City metro, you already have plenty to think about. Safe tax document delivery in Missouri should take a worry off your list, not add a task to it.

## How SafeSend Returns works from review to e-file

Here is the path from first notice to final copy. A team member is available at every step, so a question never stalls your filing.

### The team lets you know your return is ready
You receive a notification that your return is waiting. It tells you what to do next and who to call if you want to talk it through.

### Sign in through your secure login
One secure login replaces the email thread. Can't find it? Contact the team and they will get you in.

### Review your return
Your digital tax document upload is done, so now you check the result. If a number looks off, ask before you sign. Reviewing is the point, not a formality.

### Authorize with your e-signature
Signature and filing happen in the same place. Sign electronically, and the team knows you are ready to file.

### The team files your return electronically
Electronic filing follows your authorization. You do not need to mail anything or drop anything off.

### Download your final copy
You get access to a final copy of the filed return for your records. Need it later for a lender or an application? The team can help.

## Frequently Asked Questions About SafeSend Returns

**Q: What if I can't find the secure link?**
A: Check your spam or junk folder first, since notifications sometimes land there. If it isn't there, contact the team and they will resend it or help you sign in. Please don't ask for the return to be emailed as an attachment. The secure link exists specifically to avoid that.

**Q: Can I review and sign from my phone?**
A: Please confirm device support with the team when you are set up, so you are not guessing on signing day. A laptop or desktop gives you more room to read the full return line by line, and the team can help you whichever device you use.

**Q: Who sees my return?**
A: You, the team members who prepare and review it, and anyone you authorize to sign, such as a spouse on a joint return or a co-owner on an entity return. If you want someone else included, like a financial advisor, tell the team first and they will advise on how to share it.

**Q: How do I get a copy of my return later?**
A: After filing, you have access to a final copy through the secure delivery step. If you need another one later, for a mortgage lender or a loan application, contact the team and ask. They can help you get it without sending it as an email attachment.

**Q: What happens after I sign?**
A: The team files your return electronically, and you get access to a final copy. Individual filers can follow their refund with the refund tracker at /resources/refund-tracker. Business and entity filers can ask the team about next steps, such as estimated payments and year-round planning.

**Q: Are SafeSend Returns HIPAA compliant?**
A: Parker Swearngin has not made a HIPAA compliance or certification claim for this process, and we won't invent one. Tax returns generally aren't patient records. If your practice data includes patient-adjacent details, tell the team and ask how they are handled before you send anything.
