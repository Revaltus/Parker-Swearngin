## Tax planning that happens before the bill arrives

A strong year should feel like a win. Too often it ends with a tax bill nobody saw coming, because planning started the following April instead of during the year.

Business tax planning in Lee's Summit works best when it follows your real cash flow. Most pass-through owners make four estimated quarterly payments a year, and the right amount is rarely last year's number divided by four. The team projects your income, adjusts payments mid-year, and keeps you from either overpaying or landing short.

In the fall, year-end tax planning for businesses looks at timing: equipment purchases, retirement contributions, and income you can reasonably defer. Business deduction planning runs all year, so legitimate write-offs are documented when they happen. For eligible partnerships and S-corps, the team also weighs the Missouri pass-through entity tax election and whether it helps your owners.

The goal is simple. Keep the business financially healthy today, and put you on track for what you want next.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Tax planning and preparation

**Q: What does business tax preparation include?**
A: It covers returns for partnerships, corporations, S-corps, and other pass-through entities, plus secure document upload, review by seasoned tax professionals, e-signature, and electronic filing. Year-round tax advice is part of the service, so questions between filings get answered when they come up, not at the deadline.

**Q: How often should a small business pay estimated quarterly taxes?**
A: Most pass-through owners make four estimated payments a year, due in April, June, September, and January for calendar-year filers. The better question is how much. We project income against actual cash flow and adjust payments mid-year, so the amount matches the business instead of last year's return.

**Q: When does an LLC make sense as an S-corp?**
A: Usually when net profit is high enough that payroll tax savings outweigh the added cost of running payroll and filing a separate return. The answer depends on reasonable owner salary, state taxes, and your growth plans. Entity Type Analysis models both structures side by side with your real numbers before you elect anything.

**Q: Does the fixed monthly fee include tax preparation?**
A: Bundled services are priced as one fixed monthly fee, so tax work, accounting, and payroll sit together without hourly billing surprises. What's included depends on the services you choose. During your consultation, the team reviews your needs and gives you a clear monthly number before you commit to anything.

**Q: What should I do if I get an IRS notice?**
A: Don't ignore it, and don't reply before you understand it. Notices carry response deadlines, often 30 days. Send the notice to the team right away. We help gather documentation and prepare a response, and clean year-round records make that process faster and far less stressful.
